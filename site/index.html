trug

Builds Rust frontend apps for the browser: your crate compiled to WebAssembly, its Sass and the Sass its dependencies ship, written to a content-hashed dist. A failed build never replaces the last good one, and trug serve rebuilds and reloads as you edit. One binary, no Node, and moving from trunk is three renames.

Install

curl -fsSL https://udoprog.github.io/trug/install.sh | sh

Installs into ~/.local/bin (set TRUG_INSTALL_DIR to change it) on Linux and macOS. On Windows, download the zip from the releases.
